1\. Introduction

SparkTool ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and dignity of every learner, educator, administrator, and institution that engages with our platform. This Privacy Policy Statement ("Policy") explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal data in connection with the SparkTool Learning Management System and all related products and services available at sparktool.africa and through our APIs, mobile applications, and third-party integrations.

This Policy applies to all users including students, teachers, institutional administrators, parents and guardians, corporate training partners, and visitors to our website. By accessing or using SparkTool, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

This Policy is designed to comply with applicable global and regional data protection frameworks including Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, South Africa Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) 2020, Ghana Data Protection Act 2012, California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), UK GDPR, the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection (Malabo Convention), FERPA (US), COPPA (US), and other applicable national laws across our operating territories.

2\. Who We Are & Our Role

SparkTool operates as both a data controller (for data we collect directly from users who register on our platform) and a data processor (for data submitted to us by institutional clients - schools, universities, training centres - who contract our services to manage their learners).

Where we act as a processor on behalf of an institution, the institution remains the data controller responsible for its learners' and staff's personal data. Our data processing obligations in those cases are governed by Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) entered with each institutional client. If you are a learner or staff member whose data is managed through an institutional account, please also refer to your institution's own privacy policy.

Identity & registration data

- Full name, date of birth, gender, and nationality
- Student or staff identification numbers, matric numbers, and employee IDs
- Government-issued identity numbers where required for institutional compliance (National ID, NIN, NEMIS number, etc.)

Contact & account data

Academic & learning data

- Course enrolments, attendance records, and class schedules
- Assignment submissions, quiz responses, test scores, and examination results
- Learning progress, completion rates, time-on-task, and engagement metrics
- Certificates earned, transcripts, and digital credentials issued through the platform

Institutional & administrative data

- School or institution name, registration number, and accreditation details
- Staff roles, departmental affiliations, and permission levels

Technical & usage data

- IP address, device type, browser, and operating system

- Video conferencing session metadata (duration, participants) where SparkTool virtual classrooms are used
- API request logs and integration activity

4\. Special Categories of Sensitive Data

SparkTool may process certain categories of sensitive personal data in the educational context. We apply heightened protections to the following:

- Health and disability data: Where institutions record learning accommodations, disability classifications, or medical conditions that affect a learner's study plan
- Biometric data: Where institutions enable facial recognition, fingerprint attendance, or AI-proctoring for examinations - always subject to explicit, documented consent
- Ethnic or cultural background: Where institutions collect demographic data for national reporting or scholarship programmes
- Religious or belief data: Where relevant to timetabling, examination scheduling, or accommodation requests
- Children's data: Any personal data of learners under 18 years of age - governed by enhanced protections detailed in Section 9

Sensitive data is processed only with explicit consent or where there is a clear legal obligation to do so, and is subject to stricter access controls, additional encryption layers, and mandatory staff training requirements.

5\. How We Use Your Data

SparkTool processes personal data for the following purposes:

- Service delivery: Creating and maintaining user accounts, delivering course content, processing assessments, and generating academic records
- Learning analytics: Generating insights on learner progress, engagement patterns, and performance to help educators personalise instruction and improve outcomes
- Communication: Sending assignment notifications, grade alerts, course updates, institutional announcements, and - with your consent - product communications
- Certification: Issuing and verifying digital certificates, diplomas, and credentials earned through the platform
- Platform improvement: Analysing aggregated, anonymised usage data to develop new features, fix bugs, and enhance performance
- Safety & integrity: Detecting and preventing academic dishonesty, fraud, and unauthorised platform access
- Regulatory compliance: Meeting obligations to education regulators, government ministries, national examination bodies, and data protection authorities across our operating territories
- Billing & support: Processing institutional subscriptions, responding to support tickets, and resolving disputes
- Research (anonymised only): Contributing to anonymised education research where no individual can be identified - only with appropriate ethical approvals

6\. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data under one or more of the following lawful grounds, depending on the applicable jurisdiction:

- Contract performance: Processing necessary to deliver the LMS services you or your institution has contracted with us
- Legal obligation: Compliance with education regulations, examination board requirements, government reporting mandates, and data protection laws across our operating territories
- Legitimate interests: Platform security, fraud prevention, and service improvement - always balanced against the rights of individuals
- Consent: Optional analytics, marketing communications, biometric features, and research participation - withdrawable at any time without affecting core service access
- Vital interests: Where processing is necessary to protect the safety or wellbeing of a learner or staff member
- Public task: Where SparkTool supports public educational institutions carrying out functions in the public interest under applicable law

7\. Data Sharing & Disclosure

SparkTool does not sell personal data to third parties. We share data only where necessary and under appropriate legal safeguards:

Institutional clients: Schools, universities, and training organisations that have contracted SparkTool receive learner and staff data necessary to administer their academic programmes. They operate as independent data controllers for their own users.

Technology & infrastructure providers: Cloud hosting, content delivery, video streaming, cybersecurity, and email service providers - all bound by DPAs and required to maintain GDPR-equivalent standards.

Examination & accreditation bodies: National examination councils (WAEC, NECO, KNEC, Umalusi, etc.) and accreditation authorities where results or transcripts must be submitted by law or institutional mandate.

Government & regulatory bodies: Ministries of Education, national data protection authorities, law enforcement, and courts - only when legally required by valid order or statute.

Research partners: Academic or policy research institutions, but only with fully anonymised, non-identifiable datasets and applicable ethical approvals.

Professional advisors: Legal counsel, auditors, and compliance consultants operating under confidentiality obligations.

Business transfers: In the event of merger, acquisition, or restructuring, personal data may transfer subject to equivalent privacy protections and advance notice.

8\. EdTech-Specific Data Provisions

As an educational technology platform, SparkTool recognises that academic data carries unique sensitivity beyond standard personal data. We apply the following specific provisions:

- Academic records as protected data: Student grades, assessment results, disciplinary records, and learning histories are classified as highly sensitive and accessed only by authorised institutional staff and the individual learner
- No commercialisation of learner data: SparkTool will never use student academic performance data, learning behaviour, or engagement patterns for advertising, profiling for commercial purposes, or sale to third parties - under any circumstances
- Instructor intellectual property: Course content, lesson materials, and assessments uploaded by educators remain the intellectual property of the creator and/or institution; SparkTool processes them solely for service delivery
- Examination integrity data: Where online proctoring, plagiarism detection, or AI-assisted invigilation is enabled, the data generated (screen recordings, eye-tracking, keystroke logs) is processed only for examination integrity purposes and deleted within 90 days of results confirmation
- Learning analytics transparency: Learners and parents (for minors) may request a human-readable summary of the analytics data held about them and how it has influenced any automated decisions
- Portability for learners: On request, learners may export their complete academic record - transcripts, certificates, course history - in a portable format for use with other institutions or employers

9\. Children & Minor Learners

SparkTool serves learners of all ages, including children in primary and secondary education. We treat the personal data of anyone under 18 years of age with the highest level of care and apply the following enhanced protections:

Where SparkTool is deployed by a school for learners under 18, the school (as data controller) is responsible for obtaining verifiable parental or guardian consent prior to enrolment on the platform. SparkTool will not onboard minor learners on direct consumer sign-up without verified institutional involvement.

- Minor learners' profiles are set to private by default - no public-facing content, no searchable profiles
- No behavioural advertising or commercial profiling is applied to any account belonging to a learner under 18
- Parental and guardian accounts may be linked to minor learner accounts for visibility into progress and communications, subject to the institution's configuration
- Minor learners' data is never shared with any commercial third party beyond what is strictly necessary for platform operation
- We comply with COPPA (US), the UK Children's Code (Age-Appropriate Design Code), GDPR Article 8 parental consent provisions, and equivalent protections in every jurisdiction in which we operate
- Data belonging to learners under 18 is subject to a shorter default retention period (see Section 10) and is prioritised for deletion upon account closure

10\. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal requirements:

- Active learner accounts: Duration of enrolment plus 5 years post-graduation or account closure (to support transcript requests and credential verification)
- Minor learner accounts (under 18): Duration of enrolment plus 2 years, unless the learner transitions to an adult account
- Academic records & transcripts: Minimum 10 years (or the applicable statutory period under relevant education law in each territory)
- Examination and proctoring data: 90 days post-results confirmation, then securely deleted
- Staff and instructor accounts: Duration of employment or institutional contract plus 5 years
- Billing and financial records: 7 years from the end of the financial year of the transaction
- Support correspondence: 3 years from resolution
- Website analytics data: 26 months rolling (anonymised after 12 months)
- Marketing consent records: Until consent is withdrawn, plus 2 years

Upon expiry of applicable retention periods, data is securely and irreversibly deleted or anonymised using industry-standard methods. Institutions may request earlier deletion subject to applicable legal obligations.

11\. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to your jurisdiction and applicable law, you (and parents/guardians acting on behalf of minor learners) have the following rights:

- Right to access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
- Right to rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data, including academic records where errors exist
- Right to erasure: Request deletion of data where there is no legal, regulatory, or contractual obligation to retain it
- Right to restriction: Pause processing while a dispute or correction request is pending
- Right to data portability: Receive your academic record, course history, and certificates in a structured, machine-readable format
- Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes
- Rights related to automated decisions: Request human review of any significant decision made solely by automated means, including AI-generated assessment feedback or academic risk flags
- Right to withdraw consent: Revoke consent at any time for optional features (analytics, biometrics, marketing) without affecting your core access
- Right to complain: Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), Kenya Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), South Africa Information Regulator, Ghana Data Protection Commission, the EU supervisory authority in your member state, UK ICO, or any applicable authority

Submit rights requests to <privacy@sparktool.africa>. We will acknowledge within 72 hours and respond fully within 30 days (or within the shorter period required by your local law).

12\. Data Security

SparkTool employs a comprehensive, multi-layered security framework appropriate to the sensitivity of educational data:

Technical safeguards:

- AES-256 encryption for all data at rest; TLS 1.3 for all data in transit
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) enforced for all administrator and instructor accounts; available for all learners
- Role-based access control (RBAC) - academic data is accessible only to authorised individuals within the relevant institution
- Real-time intrusion detection, web application firewall (WAF), and DDoS mitigation
- Regular independent penetration testing and vulnerability assessments
- Automated security patching and continuous dependency monitoring
- Separate data environments for each institutional client (logical data isolation)

Operational safeguards:

- All staff with access to personal data undergo mandatory annual data protection training and background checks
- Incident response plan with 72-hour breach notification to relevant supervisory authorities (GDPR/NDPA compliant) and affected institutions
- A designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) oversees all privacy compliance activities
- Privacy-by-design and privacy-by-default principles are embedded in all product development

13\. International Data Transfers

SparkTool operates across multiple African countries and internationally. Personal data may be transferred to, stored, or processed in countries outside your country of residence. Where such transfers occur, we ensure appropriate safeguards through:

- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission for transfers to non-adequate countries
- Adequacy decisions where recognised (e.g., EU-UK)
- Compliance with POPIA Section 72 for cross-border transfers from South Africa
- Compliance with NDPA 2023 transfer provisions for Nigeria-origin data
- Compliance with Kenya DPA Section 49 for cross-border transfers from Kenya
- Binding processor agreements that mandate jurisdiction-appropriate protections

Where local data residency requirements apply (e.g., certain Nigerian government-affiliated institutions), SparkTool can provision data storage within country on request, subject to institutional contract terms.

14\. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our platform uses cookies and similar technologies to deliver functionality and understand usage. We use only the following categories:

- Strictly necessary cookies: Session management, authentication, and platform security - required for the platform to function; cannot be disabled
- Functional cookies: Remembering preferences (language, accessibility settings, last course visited) to personalise your experience
- Performance & analytics cookies: Understanding how learners and administrators use the platform to improve it - used only with your consent; data is aggregated and anonymised
- Marketing cookies: Used only on our public website (sparktool.africa) and only with explicit consent; never placed within the learner platform environment

Manage cookie preferences at any time via the Cookie Preference Centre accessible from any page footer, or through your browser settings. Disabling non-essential cookies has no effect on access to learning content or academic functions.

15\. Institutional Responsibilities

Schools, universities, and training organisations that deploy SparkTool for their learners and staff carry the following responsibilities as independent data controllers:

- Obtain all necessary consents from learners (and parents/guardians for minors) before enrolling them on the SparkTool platform
- Maintain their own compliant privacy policy that discloses SparkTool as a data processor
- Ensure that only the minimum necessary personal data is submitted to SparkTool to fulfil the educational purpose
- Promptly notify SparkTool of any changes to the legal basis for processing learner data, including withdrawal of consent
- Configure platform permission levels appropriately to restrict staff access to only the learner data relevant to their role
- Notify SparkTool immediately upon becoming aware of any data breach, security incident, or unauthorised disclosure involving platform data

Institutional clients must enter a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with SparkTool before activating any account. Non-compliance with these responsibilities may result in suspension of institutional access.

16\. Artificial Intelligence & Automated Processing

SparkTool uses AI-assisted features including personalised learning recommendations, automated assessment feedback, plagiarism detection, and at-risk learner identification. We apply the following principles to all automated processing:

- Transparency: Learners and educators are always informed when AI has generated or influenced a recommendation, assessment flag, or learning pathway suggestion
- Human oversight: No consequential academic decision (grade, enrolment suspension, examination referral) is made solely by automated means without human review by a qualified educator or administrator
- Fairness: Our AI models are regularly audited for bias, particularly in relation to language, ethnicity, gender, and socioeconomic background relevant to African learner populations
- Right to explanation: Learners may request a plain-language explanation of any AI-influenced outcome that affects their academic standing
- No surveillance profiling: Behavioural data captured during learning sessions is used only to improve educational outcomes - never to build commercial profiles or for purposes unrelated to education

Continued use of SparkTool after the effective date of an updated Policy constitutes acceptance of the revised terms. Institutional administrators who object to material changes should contact us to discuss transition options.
